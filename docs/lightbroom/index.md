# LightBroom

LightBroom brings light to your sweeping with **18 motion-responsive LEDs** built into the broom head. The lights react as you move and sweep.

## Broom Basics

- [Replacing Sleeves](replacing-sleeves.md): fit fresh fabric while reusing the foam and tension plates.

## Using LightBroom

- [Getting Started](getting-started.md): turn your broom on and off, sweep, and wake it after automatic shutoff.
- [Using LightBroom](using-lightbroom.md): turn it on, let the light flow with gravity, and sweep to make it sparkle.
- [Choosing Colours](colours-and-effects.md): select rainbow or a single colour.
- [Charging](charging.md): read the battery bar and charging indicators.
