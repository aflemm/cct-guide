# Getting Started

## 1. Charge your broom

Connect the USB-C port to a USB power supply. See [Charging](charging.md) to read the battery indicators.

## 2. Turn it on and sweep

Press and release the button. After the startup animation and brief battery display, tilt the head to see the light flow with gravity. Sweep to build brightness and sparkle.

Follow [Using LightBroom](using-lightbroom.md) for everyday operation, turning it off, and waking it after automatic shutoff.

## 3. Choose your colours

Use [Choosing Colours](colours-and-effects.md) to select rainbow or a single colour. Your choice is saved for next time.

For broom care, see [Replacing Sleeves](replacing-sleeves.md). Read [Competition Use](competition-compliance/index.md) before using LightBroom in an event requiring certified equipment.
