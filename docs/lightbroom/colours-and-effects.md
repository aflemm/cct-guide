# Choosing Colours

## Choose a colour

1. Wait for the startup battery display to finish, or dismiss it with a short button press.
2. **Press once** to show the current colour selection.
3. **Press again while the selection is visible** to move to the next option. Each press keeps the selection display open for another **2.5 seconds**.
4. Stop pressing when you reach the option you want. Normal lighting resumes when the display closes.

The options cycle through **red → yellow → green → teal → blue → purple → rainbow → red**.

For a single colour, one light in the selection bar shows that colour and the others are dim white. For rainbow, all six lights show different colours.

Your selection is saved and restored the next time you turn on the broom. Rainbow is the initial default.
