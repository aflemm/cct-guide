# SmartBeam

**SmartBeam** uses laser beams across the sheet to measure rock speeds and split times. Connect your sensors to the SmartBeam app to set up measurements, time throws, and review results.

[Get started →](getting-started.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

Read [Laser Safety](laser-safety.md) before switching on your sensors.

## Prepare your system

- [System Overview](system-overview.md): understand how sensors, reflectors, and the app work together.
- [SmartBeam App](smartbeam-app.md): get the app ready on your phone or tablet.
- [Charging](charging.md): charge your sensors and read the battery indicators.
- [Connecting](connecting-smartbeam.md): connect your sensors to the app.

## Set up and time a throw

- [Setting up Beams](setting-up-beams/index.md): position and align sensors and reflectors on the ice.
- [Choosing Measurements](choosing-measurements.md): decide which speeds and split times to measure.
- [Arming and Timing](arming-and-timing/index.md): prepare the system and time a rock.
- [Understanding Results](understanding-results.md): read your speeds and split times.

## Make practice flow

- [Arming Modes](arming-modes.md): choose how the system prepares for the next throw.
- [History and Data](history-and-data.md): review your timing history.
- [Saved Devices](saved-devices.md): manage the sensors you use.
- [RockArm](rockarm.md): keep your timing display in view during practice.
