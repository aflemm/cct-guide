# Laser Safety

SmartBeam contains a laser. Read these precautions before switching it on.

- **Never look directly into the laser beam.**
- **Keep SmartBeam out of reach of children.**
- Place SmartBeam flat on the ice before switching it on.
- Switch SmartBeam off before picking it up or moving it. Do not carry it while the laser is on.
