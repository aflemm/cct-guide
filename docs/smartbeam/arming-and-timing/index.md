# Arming and Timing

**Arming prepares the system for a rock; the rock starts the timing.** Once armed, SmartBeam follows the beams in order along the selected throwing direction.

Before arming, [connect and set up your beams](../setting-up-beams/index.md). Every beam participating in the throw must be clear and established.

## How to know the system is ready

Use the lights to understand the system without looking at your phone. When ready, the nearest participating beam shows <span class="led-colour" data-led-colour="yellow">yellow</span> and the waiting beams farther down the sheet show <span class="led-colour" data-led-colour="purple">purple</span>. The app shows **READY**.

These are the system-state LEDs at the ends of the top light-bar. The yellow beam is the **hot beam**: the next one the system expects the rock to break.

## Prepare, deliver, repeat

1. **Choose how to arm.** Use [Arming Modes](../arming-modes.md) for Manual, Automatic, or First Beam. First Beam is best for solo practice: sliding back to the hack starts arming for your next throw.
2. **Wait for readiness, then deliver.** Timing begins when the rock breaks the first beam. As it travels, the next beam becomes hot and completed beams turn <span class="led-colour" data-led-colour="green">green</span>.
3. **Review the throw.** Results appear as measurements complete and are saved to **History**. Your arming mode determines how you prepare for the next rock.

See [Timing Lights and Status](timing-lights-and-status.md) for the full light sequence and app states, or [Cancelling and Timeouts](../cancelling-and-timeouts.md) when you need to stop or a throw does not finish.
