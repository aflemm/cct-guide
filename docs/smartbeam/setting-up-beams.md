## Match the setup in the app

Choose your device to see the relevant controls and screenshots.

=== "iPhone"

    1. Open the **SmartBeam** tab with your units connected.
    2. Tap the location on the sheet where you placed a beam, then choose the matching SmartBeam from **Place Beam Here**.
    3. Repeat for each beam in your setup.
    4. Tap the direction arrow to match the direction you will throw.

    To change the setup, open a placed beam's menu to move it or choose **Remove From Setup**. Removing a beam from the setup is separate from disconnecting it.

## Check your setup

- Each beam's location in the app matches its physical location.
- The selected units match the colours of the hardware you placed.
- The throwing direction arrow points the way your rock will travel.
