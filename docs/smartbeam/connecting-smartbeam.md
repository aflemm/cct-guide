# Connecting

Connect your SmartBeams to the SmartBeam app using its Bluetooth scanner.

## Before you start

Turn on your SmartBeams and enable Bluetooth on your phone. Allow the app to use Bluetooth when prompted.

=== "iPhone"

    Allow SmartBeam's Bluetooth access when iPhone asks for permission.

=== "Android"

    Allow **Nearby devices** access. If the app shows **Bluetooth Access Needed**, tap **Continue** and allow the permission.

    If the scanner shows **Permission Required**, tap **Open Settings** and enable **Nearby devices** permission for SmartBeam, then return to the app.

## Open the scanner

On first launch, the welcome flow includes a scanning step where you can connect your units.

To open the scanner again, use the button at the top right of the app:

=== "iPhone"

    Tap the antenna icon with radio waves.

    ![SmartBeam tab toolbar on iPhone, with the antenna-shaped scanner button at the top right](../assets/images/smartbeam-app/open-scanner-iphone.png){ width="480" }

=== "Android"

    Tap the Bluetooth icon.

## Connect your SmartBeams

1. Find a unit in the **Discovered** list. Each row shows its name and case colour to help you choose the right one.
2. Tap **Connect** beside it.
3. Wait for the unit to appear in the **Connected** list.
4. Repeat for each SmartBeam you want to use.

When all the units you need appear in the connected list, close the scanner using the **×** button.

## Reconnecting

The app remembers units you connect to and attempts to reconnect automatically when it discovers them again. Turn on your SmartBeams and open the app, then check the scanner's connected list.

If a unit appears in the discovered list instead, tap **Connect** beside it.

## Disconnecting

Open the scanner and tap **Disconnect** beside a connected unit. This leaves the unit saved in the app, but stops automatic reconnection for the current app session. To reconnect it during that session, find it in the discovered list and tap **Connect**.

## Next step

Once your units are connected, follow [Setting up Beams](setting-up-beams.md) to assign them to locations on the sheet.
