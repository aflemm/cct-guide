# SmartBeam App

The SmartBeam app is free and connects to your SmartBeams to display split times and rock speeds.

## Get the app

=== "iPhone"

    Download SmartBeam from the App Store, then open it to begin setup.

    [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/smartbeam/id6758161520){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

=== "Android"

    The Android app is available through the Play Store beta program. Email us to request access.

    [Request Android beta access](mailto:smartbeam-android@smartbroom.ca){ .md-button }

## Find your way around

The iPhone app has four tabs:

| Tab | What you do here |
| --- | --- |
| **SmartBeam** | Set up beam locations and throwing direction, arm the system, and see live timing results. |
| **History** | Review saved rocks and filter them by date. |
| **Settings** | Manage devices, change timing preferences, and find firmware updates and data tools. |
| **About** | Find the user guide, contact information, and announcements. |
