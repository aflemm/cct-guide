# Getting started

Connect your SmartBeams, set up the sheet, and time your first rock.

--8<-- "documentation-status.md"

## 1. Get the app and charge your SmartBeams

Install the SmartBeam app and make sure your units are charged.

=== "iPhone"

    Download SmartBeam from the App Store, then open it to begin setup.

    [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/smartbeam/id6758161520){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

=== "Android"

    The Android app is available through the Play Store beta program. Email us to request access.

    [Request Android beta access](mailto:smartbeam-android@smartbroom.ca){ .md-button }


[More about the SmartBeam App →](smartbeam-app.md)

[More about Charging →](charging.md)

## 2. Turn on and connect

Power on your SmartBeams and open the app. On first launch, the welcome flow helps you scan and connect. Tap **Connect** beside each unit you want to use.

<div class="guide-wireframe" role="img" aria-label="Screenshot placeholder: scanner showing discovered and connected SmartBeams">
  <strong>App screenshot</strong>
  <span>Scanner · Discovered devices · Connect · Connected devices</span>
</div>

**Ready to continue:** Your units appear in the connected devices list.

[More about Connecting →](connecting-smartbeam.md)

## 3. Set up your beams

Place and align the hardware on the sheet, then assign each connected SmartBeam to its matching location in the app. Set the arrow to your throwing direction.

[More about Setting up Beams →](setting-up-beams.md)

## 4. Arm and time a rock

For this first run, select **Manual** arming and tap **Arm**. Deliver a rock through your configured beams. The app displays the timing sequence as it happens.

<div class="guide-wireframe" role="img" aria-label="Screenshot placeholder: Manual arming selected with the Arm control highlighted">
  <strong>App screenshot</strong>
  <span>Manual mode · Arm button · Timing state</span>
</div>

**Ready to continue:** The run completes and a result appears.

[More about Arming and Timing →](arming-and-timing.md)

## 5. Read your first result

The results available depend on your beam setup. Read the split times and rock speeds shown, then open **History** to review saved rocks.
