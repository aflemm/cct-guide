# Getting started

Follow these steps to get your SmartBeams ready and time your first rock.

## 1. Get the app

Start with the [SmartBeam App](smartbeam-app.md) page to get the app for your phone and find your way around.

## 2. Get your SmartBeams ready

[Charge your SmartBeams](charging.md), then [connect them to the app](connecting-smartbeam.md).

## 3. Set up your beams

Follow [Setting up Beams](setting-up-beams.md) to match your setup on the sheet with the setup in the app.

## 4. Time your first rock

Follow [Arming and Timing](arming-and-timing.md) to start a timing sequence.

## 5. Read your results

See [Understanding Results](understanding-results.md) for help reading your first result.

Need a hand? [Contact Curling Tools](../contact.md).
