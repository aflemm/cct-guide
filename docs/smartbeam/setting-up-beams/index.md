# Setting up Beams

Your setup has two parts: **where the beams cross the ice** and **where you assign them in the app**. Keep them matched so each result describes the right part of the throw.

## Set up in four steps

1. **Choose your measurements.** Decide which splits and speeds you want to capture together. See [Choosing Measurements](../choosing-measurements.md) for placement options.
2. **Place and align the hardware.** Put each sensor opposite its reflector across the sheet. The arrow-shaped beam-state LED shows <span class="led-colour" data-led-colour="green">green</span> when the beam is established. Read [Laser Safety](../laser-safety.md) before setup.
3. **Match the app to the ice.** [Connect your SmartBeams](../connecting-smartbeam.md), then assign each unit to its physical location on the sheet diagram.
4. **Check the throwing direction.** Point the app's arrow toward the receiving end. Reverse it when changing ends.

Use [Beam Alignment](../beam-alignment.md) to check or adjust the physical beam, and [Managing Beams in the App](managing-beams-in-the-app.md) for assigning, identifying, moving, or removing units.

Once the beams are clear and the app matches the ice, continue to [Arming and Timing](../arming-and-timing/index.md).
