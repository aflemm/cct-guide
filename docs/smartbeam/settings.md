# Settings

Open the **Settings** tab to manage connected SmartBeams, adjust timing and display preferences, and access your saved data.

## SmartBeam Devices

Connected units appear at the top with their names, case colours, serial numbers, battery indicators, signal strengths, and firmware versions. Tap a unit to open its details.

- **Update Centre** checks connected units for firmware updates. See [Firmware Updates](firmware-updates.md).
- **Saved SmartBeam Devices** manages remembered units and automatic reconnection. See [Saved Devices](saved-devices.md).
- **Connected Device Messages**, when present, contains messages relevant to your connected units.

The scanner button at the top right opens the Bluetooth scanner. See [Connecting](connecting-smartbeam.md).

## System Arming

Choose **Manual**, **Automatic**, or **First Beam**. Automatic mode also has a delay control for the wait before re-arming.

See [Arming Modes](arming-modes.md) for the controls and how each mode works.

## Display

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Rock speed display locations** | Chooses which groups of speed readings are shown. All speeds are still measured, saved, and exported. |
| **Display times in sheet order** | Arranges results to match their positions on the sheet. Turn it off to use capture order. |
| **Hide previous times and speeds** | Hides the smaller previous readings beneath the main result. Saved results remain in History. |

See [Understanding Results](understanding-results.md) for reading the display and selecting speed locations.

## Saved Setups

**Automatically Restore Last Setup** restores beam placements by matching the colours in your last used setup as units connect. If a colour needs you to choose between units, place that beam yourself. Automatic restoration stops when you start timing a rock.

Always check that the restored assignments match the hardware's actual positions before arming. See [Managing Beams in the App](setting-up-beams/managing-beams-in-the-app.md#match-the-setup-in-the-app).

## Active Beam Locations

| Setting | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Far tee-line and back-line** | Includes assigned beams at the receiving-end tee and back lines in the timing sequence. Useful for hit-speed practice; the rock must reach those positions for the sequence to finish normally. |
| **Between hog-line locations** | Makes the centre-sheet and beyond-hog positions available for placing beams, including post-release speed measurements. |

See [Setting up Beams](setting-up-beams/index.md) for choosing physical and app locations.

## Power Saving

**Keep screen active** prevents the iPhone from dimming and automatically locking while SmartBeam is open and active.

The LED dimming control chooses the battery percentage below which SmartBeam's indicator LEDs dim to **50% brightness**. The end options are **Never** and **Always** dim LEDs. This changes brightness, rather than the meaning of each colour; see [LED Indication](led-indication.md).

## Data

**Data Management** shows stored-record information and provides CSV export and erase options. See [History and Data](history-and-data.md).

**Reset all settings to default** restores app preferences after confirmation. It does not erase saved timing results; deletion is a separate action in Data Management.
