# Settings

Use the **Settings** tab for app preferences. To change a connected broom's power-saving options, tap its name on the connected-broom screen.

## App preferences

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Units** | Displays force in kilograms or pounds. |
| **Automatic Connection** | Reconnects to saved SmartBrooms discovered nearby. |
| **Proximity Connection** | Connects to a nearby SmartBroom with a strong enough signal, including an unsaved broom. |
| **Blackout Mode** | Hides live sweep data from view. |

For a deliberate one-broom-per-window setup on iPad, connect each broom from its intended window. See [Multiple Windows on iPad](ipad-windows.md).

## Saved devices and data

**Saved SmartBroom Devices** lists remembered brooms. Disconnect when you want to end a connection but keep the saved device.

Forgetting a bonded broom removes its remembered key and can make data still on the broom unrecoverable. Read [Bonding and Ownership](bonding.md#release-or-forget-a-broom) before forgetting or changing ownership.

**SmartBroom 4 Data Management** contains saved-data information, download history, and erasure options. See [Sessions and Downloads](sessions.md).

## Broom power saving

Open the connected broom's details to find **Power Saving**:

- **Shake to wake:** allows the broom to wake when shaken after an automatic power-down.
- **Sweep idle sleep:** powers down after the selected time without a recorded sweep.
- **Motion idle sleep:** powers down after the selected time without movement.

These timeouts are bypassed while Bluetooth is connected. For [Background Recording](background-recording.md), choose settings that suit your use and check that the broom is awake before sweeping.
