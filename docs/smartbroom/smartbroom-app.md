# SmartBroom App

The SmartBroom app connects to your broom, shows live sweeping measurements, and downloads stored sweeping data for review on iPhone or iPad.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/smartbroom/id1453540933){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## Compatibility

| Device | Minimum operating system |
| --- | --- |
| **iPhone** | iOS **17** or later. |
| **iPad** | iPadOS **17** or later. |

Enable Bluetooth and allow the app to use it. See [Connecting](connecting-smartbroom.md) for permissions and setup. On iPad, you can use [multiple windows with separate brooms](ipad-windows.md).

## Find your way around

| Tab | What you do here |
| --- | --- |
| **SmartBroom** | Connect a broom, start live sessions, prepare background collection, and download saved data. |
| **Review** | Browse recorded sessions and individual sweeps. Choose **SmartBroom 4** in the model selector. |
| **Sweepers** | Manage the people whose sweeping you record. |
| **Settings** | Choose units, connection preferences, display options, and data management. |
| **About** | Find product information, announcements, and support. |

## Live or background?

| | Live sweeping | Background recording |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where measurements go | Streamed to the app during a live sweep. | Saved in the broom, then downloaded to the app. |
| Phone connection | Keep the app connected for live feedback. | Not required while sweeping once the broom is prepared. |
| Ownership | An unclaimed broom can be used live. An already bonded broom must be unlocked with its owner's key. | Claim the broom first; the bonding key is needed to download its stored data. |
| Useful for | Practice with immediate feedback and named sweeps. | Games or practice where you want to put the phone away. |

Background recording means recording **on the broom**, rather than keeping the phone app running in the background. Live transmission and local background storage are different operating modes, not two simultaneous copies of the same sweep.

Start with [Connecting](connecting-smartbroom.md), then choose [Live Sweeping](using-smartbroom.md) or [Background Recording](background-recording.md).

For individual force samples and CSV capture, see [Raw Live Data](raw-live-data.md).
