# Sessions and Downloads

Live sweeps and downloaded background recordings can be reviewed in the app. A **session** groups sweeps; a **download** is a saved collection of records transferred from the broom.

## Find a session

1. Open **Review** and choose **SmartBroom 4**.
2. Open **All Sessions** or select a month. Use the session-type filter to narrow the list if needed.
3. Open a session to compare its sweeps. When several sweepers are included, select a sweeper to filter the view.
4. Open a sweep to see its charts, individual statistics, sweeper, and notes.

You can edit session details and a sweep's sweeper or notes. These are useful for identifying practice drills and making later comparisons. For the measurements themselves, see [Understanding Results](understanding-results.md).

## Find a background download

Connect the broom and open **Download History** from its connected screen. Entries show the download date, stroke count, and dates covered by the records.

To see downloads across all brooms, open **Settings → SmartBroom 4 Data Management → All SmartBroom 4 Downloads**.

A download's date can differ from the dates of the sweeps it contains: you may download records after several games or practices. Follow [Background Recording](background-recording.md) to collect and transfer them.

## Export or remove data

Use the **share button** in a sweep's detail view to export its CSV. Download History also has a share button for exporting the records in a download as CSV.

Data Management shows the saved SmartBroom 4 data and provides date-based or complete erasure options. Deleting a download also removes its derived data. **Export anything you want to keep before deleting it.** Records already downloaded and acknowledged may no longer be on the broom for another transfer.
