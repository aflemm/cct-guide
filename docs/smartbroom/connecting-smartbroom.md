# Connecting

## Connect to your SmartBroom

1. Switch on your SmartBroom with its power button.
2. Open the app's **SmartBroom** tab and allow Bluetooth access when prompted. Bluetooth must be enabled on the phone or iPad.
3. Find your broom under **Discovered** and tap **Connect**.
4. Wait for the connected-broom screen to finish checking the device. It shows collection options, battery level, ownership, and saved data.

If Bluetooth access was denied, use **Open SmartBroom Settings** to allow it in the device's Settings app. If scanning has stopped, use the scan control to look again.

## Automatic connections

In **Settings → Connection**:

- **Automatic Connection** reconnects to remembered SmartBrooms when they are discovered.
- **Proximity Connection** can connect to a broom brought very close to the phone or iPad.

For choosing between several brooms, use the discovered list. Match the connected device's name and serial number before collecting data. For separate broom controls on one iPad, see [Multiple Windows on iPad](ipad-windows.md).

## Connection and ownership are different

Connecting opens communication with the broom. **Claiming** establishes the key used to access saved background data. The app tries a remembered key automatically on reconnection.

If the broom shows **Locked — key unavailable**, reconnect using the owner's app or a device with the same key. Do not use **Erase & Claim** as a routine connection fix: it deletes data stored on the broom. See [Bonding and Ownership](bonding.md).

## Disconnect

Use **Disconnect** on the connected-broom screen to end the Bluetooth connection. This does not release ownership or erase stored sweeps.

For recording without a connection, prepare [Background Recording](background-recording.md) first and check the broom's recording lights after disconnecting.
