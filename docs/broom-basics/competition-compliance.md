# Competition Compliance

The following Curling Tools brooms are World Curling approved when fitted with **BlueFoam 2** and a **competition fabric sleeve**:

- **SmartBroom 3**
- **SmartBroom 4**
- **CoreBroom**

## Check your broom before competition

1. **Check the broom model.** Use one of the approved brooms listed above.
2. **Check the sleeve's product code.** A competition sleeve has a valid World Curling product code on the underside of its sewn-in label. A recreational sleeve does not meet the competition-sleeve requirement.
3. **Check the foam version.** Use BlueFoam 2, identified by the marking **20-056**.

## When replacing components

Keep the approved combination together when changing a sleeve or foam. Fitting a competition sleeve alone does not make an unapproved broom or foam competition compliant.

Follow [Replacing Sleeves](replacing-sleeves.md) for the sleeve-changing procedure. Check the current equipment requirements for your competition before playing.
